Delete your KatchUp account

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open KatchUp and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Account & AI, tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password (or confirm with Google), type DELETE, then tap Delete my account.

It takes effect straight away. Tip: use Settings → Export backup first if you want to keep a copy of your notes.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address on your account (or tell us which Google account you used to sign in) with the subject “KatchUp account deletion”.

Email a deletion request

We may ask you to confirm it is your account. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your KatchUp account and sign-in (your email address and, for Google sign-in, your name).
  • Everything synced to our cloud database: decks, flashcards, notes, courses, mind maps, practice-test results and progress.
  • When you delete from inside the app, also all KatchUp data saved on that device, including your saved Gemini API key. If you ask us by email, remove the app data on your device by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.

What we cannot delete

  • Backup files you exported to your own device or storage.
  • Content that Google processed when you used AI features with your own Gemini API key. That is governed by Google’s terms, and you can manage it through your Google account.
