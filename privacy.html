Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short
  • KatchUp is for people aged 18 or older.
  • Your study content lives on your device. If you are signed in, a copy syncs to our cloud database so you can use KatchUp on other devices.
  • AI features send only what you choose to Google’s Gemini service, using your own API key. We never see your key.
  • There are no ads, no tracking tools and we do not sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time: Settings → Delete account.

1. Who we are

KatchUp is a study app (flashcards, notes, courses, mind maps and practice tests) run by an individual developer in South Africa. Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) we are the “responsible party” for the information described here. You can reach us at kabomoagi14@gmail.com.

2. Who can use KatchUp

You must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete that account and its data.

3. Information we collect and why

Account detailsYour email address and, if you use Google sign-in, the name and email on your Google account. Passwords are handled by Google’s Firebase Authentication; we never see them. We use this to sign you in and keep your data tied to you.
Study contentDecks, flashcards, notes, courses, mind maps, practice-test results, progress, streaks and coins. This is stored on your device. When you are signed in, a copy is synced to our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore) so you can continue on another device.
SettingsTheme, sound and accessibility choices. Kept on your device only.
Your Gemini API keySaved only on your device. It is not sent to our servers. It is sent only to Google when you use an AI feature.
Files, photos and audio you addProcessed on your device. Downloaded practice papers and music stay on your device. If you use an AI feature on a file, photo or recording, it is sent to Google (see section 4).
MicrophoneUsed only after you tap record. Live speech-to-text is done by your browser’s or phone’s speech service. For example, Chrome may send audio to Google for this.
NotificationsIf you allow them, study reminders are shown on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising ID. KatchUp currently shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools. If that changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

4. AI features and Google

When you use an AI feature (for example making flashcards, courses, mind maps or practice tests, or transcribing a recording), the content you choose to send goes directly from your device to Google’s Gemini API using your own API key. It does not pass through our servers. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to that content.

For unpaid use, Google says it may use submitted content to improve its products and that human reviewers may read it. Please do not submit confidential or sensitive personal information, yours or anyone else’s. Google also requires API key holders to be 18 or older.

5. Other services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in and cloud sync.
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs: load fonts and code libraries. They may see your IP address and device details when they do.
  • Free web relay services (allorigins.win, corsproxy.io, codetabs.com): used only by “Find practice tests”, and only when a paper cannot be downloaded directly. The web address of the paper passes through one of them. We do not send them your account details.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

Account and cloud data are kept until you delete them. Data on your device stays until you clear it or delete your account. Once you delete your account, we remove your cloud data and your sign-in. Backup files you exported yourself, and anything Google already processed under your own API key, are outside our control.

7. Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. If you cannot open the app, see how to request deletion.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you can object to how we use it. You can download a copy of your content any time from Settings → Export backup. To use any of these rights, email us. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

9. Security

Data travels over HTTPS and your cloud data is stored under your own account ID in Google’s Firebase. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell us if you suspect someone else has used your account.

10. Where your data is processed

Google’s servers may be outside South Africa. By using KatchUp you understand that your information may be processed there.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: kabomoagi14@gmail.com.
